About Village Voice

Village Voice is a Rural Infrastructure Complaint Management System developed to help villagers report infrastructure issues easily. Users can submit complaints regarding roads, drainage, water supply, sanitation, street lights and other village problems using English, Telugu, images and voice recordings. The concerned Sarpanch can monitor and resolve complaints efficiently.
